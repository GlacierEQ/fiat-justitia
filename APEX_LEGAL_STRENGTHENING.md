# APEX Legal Strengthening

This repository is an additive APEX legal execution surface. Existing case data, provenance, chronology, allegations, authorities, branches, and stronger working mechanisms are preserved.

Binding direction: `OPERATOR_INTENT` and `MAXIMUM_COHERENT_ADVANCE`. Companion doctrine: `GlacierEQ/apex-control-plane/legal/APEX_LEGAL_STRENGTHENING_V1.md`.

Required behavior:
- full-field legal issue spotting before pruning;
- preserve allegation/theory labels through element-by-element audit;
- map actor -> act/omission -> source -> element -> prejudice/injury -> remedy;
- maintain exact chronology, notice, service, filing, deadline, custody/control, and post-event record timing;
- apply the law/rule version governing the event or procedural act;
- preserve contradictions and adverse records for reconciliation/impeachment instead of silently choosing one;
- distinguish fact, source statement, inference, allegation, theory, contradiction, proof gap, remedy, and next action;
- expose proof gaps as retrieval/discovery/preservation targets;
- preserve individual, supervisory, entity, policy/custom, ratification, and failure-to-intervene chains where supportable;
- keep damages, fees, sanctions, injunctive/declaratory, correction/removal, access-restoration, preservation, and administrative remedies visible until the filing target is chosen;
- never fabricate authority, quotation, holding, docket fact, actor identity, or event;
- generated work is not verified work; material filing positions remain source-grounded;
- no summary, governance artifact, or bookkeeping substitute for requested substantive legal execution.

Strengthening means more coverage, provenance, temporal accuracy, evidentiary traceability, contradiction detection, actor accountability, remedy coverage, procedural leverage, retrieval power, drafting quality, reliability, or verified execution capability without losing prior valid gains.
