# Easy Exhibit: Motion to Dismiss

**Rule:** Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(b)(6)
**Agent Assigned:** procedure-agent
**Proof Class:** filed

## I. Introduction
Defendant hereby moves to dismiss Plaintiff's complaint for failure to state a claim upon which relief can be granted.

## II. Standard of Review
Under Rule 12(b)(6), a complaint must contain sufficient factual matter, accepted as true, to "state a claim to relief that is plausible on its face." *Ashcroft v. Iqbal*.

## III. Argument
Plaintiff's complaint fails to allege any facts demonstrating that Defendant owed a duty of care, a necessary element of the negligence claim. The allegations are merely conclusory statements without factual support.

## IV. Conclusion
For the foregoing reasons, Defendant requests that the Court dismiss the complaint in its entirety with prejudice.
