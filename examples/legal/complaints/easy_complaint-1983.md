# Easy Exhibit: 42 U.S.C. § 1983 Civil Rights Complaint

**Jurisdiction:** Federal Question (28 U.S.C. § 1331)
**Rule:** 42 U.S.C. § 1983
**Agent Assigned:** civil-rights-agent
**Proof Class:** filed

## I. Introduction
This is a standard civil rights complaint for deprivation of constitutional rights under color of state law.

## II. Parties
1. Plaintiff is a resident of [State].
2. Defendant is a state actor employed by [Agency].

## III. Statement of Facts
On [Date], Defendant, acting under color of state law, unlawfully deprived Plaintiff of their rights secured by the Fourth Amendment by engaging in unreasonable search and seizure without probable cause.

## IV. Causes of Action
**Count I: 42 U.S.C. § 1983 - Fourth Amendment Violation**
Defendant's actions were objectively unreasonable and caused direct harm to the Plaintiff.

## V. Prayer for Relief
WHEREFORE, Plaintiff requests compensatory damages, attorneys' fees pursuant to 42 U.S.C. § 1988, and any other relief the Court deems just.
